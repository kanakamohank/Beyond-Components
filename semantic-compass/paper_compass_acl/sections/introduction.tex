\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

A line of mechanistic-interpretability work establishes that
individual singular directions of attention-head weights carry
interpretable signal. \citet{elhage2021mathematical} formalise the
OV decomposition $W_{OV} = W_V W_O$ that factors a head into its
read- and write-side maps; \citet{millidge2022svd} show that
singular directions of $W_{OV}$ and $W_{QK}$ frequently decode
through $W_U$ to coherent vocabulary; \citet{ahmad2025beyond}
extract sparse direction-level circuits via learnable masks over
the singular values for the Gender Pronoun (GP), IOI, and
Greater-Than tasks.

Several phenomena that language models exhibit do not reduce to
single-axis binary contrasts. Temporal units (\emph{month},
\emph{hour}, \emph{year}) form a four-way categorical structure;
gender-marking heads can split singular vs.\ plural and masculine
vs.\ feminine in non-orthogonal ways
\citep{engels2024not,park2024linearrep}; the same categorical axis
can be \emph{written} at one head and \emph{steered} at another.
We propose that reading attention-head weights as
multi-dimensional geometric objects --- starting with 2D planes
spanned by pairs of singular directions --- captures these
phenomena directly. On such a plane an angle parameterises a
continuous family of causal interventions, a radius parameterises
magnitude, and the second harmonic separates two-way antipodal
axes from four-way dials.

\paragraph{The compass.}
Given $W_{OV} = U \Sigma V^\top$ and writing $u_i := V^\top_{[i,:]}$,
a \emph{semantic compass} is an ordered pair $(u_i, u_j)$ that passes
three tests: (i) vocabulary decoding through $W_U$ assigns antipodal
categorical content at the two poles; (ii) the residual injection
$v(\theta, \alpha) = \alpha\sigma(\cos\theta\, u_i + \sin\theta\,
u_j)$, applied at the head's own layer on the last token, produces a
sinusoidal logit response whose phase is stable across $\alpha$;
(iii) cycling $\theta$ from $0$ to $2\pi$ closes onto identical decode
vocabulary, with the antipode disjoint. The recipe is probe-free and
weight-localized.

\paragraph{Why the geometry matters.}
Reading singular directions individually is a one-dimensional view
of a higher-dimensional object. Two phenomena in our data are
invisible to that view and surface only once we treat the plane as
the unit of analysis.

\emph{Four-way categorical structure.} On the Phi-3 L24H17 plane,
a four-token probe at $\theta \in \{0^\circ, 90^\circ, 180^\circ,
270^\circ\}$ produces a strong second harmonic ($A_2/A_1 = 0.32$
at $\alpha = 10$); the same probe on the Phi-3 L24H28 plane gives
$A_2/A_1 \leq 0.06$. The first encodes a four-way temporal dial
(month / hour / year / upon); the second carries two superposed
two-way axes. Reading either plane's two singular directions one
at a time gives the same vocabulary in both cases; the harmonic
geometry distinguishes them.

\emph{Storage versus steering as a quantitative dissociation.} The
$\alpha$-sweep slope and the in-plane causal-patch magnitude
dissociate across heads (\cref{tab:storage}). Phi-3 L24H10 has the
largest in-plane signal but a small sweep slope; L28H1 has the
reverse profile. The same gender axis sits at two distinct
operating points in the same model.

\paragraph{A near-causal plane evidenced across architectures.}
Five independent tests on every primary head support the plane
as a near-causal axis: a sinusoidal $\alpha$-sweep with phase
drift $\leq 3^\circ$, $2\pi$-cyclic decode that closes onto
antipodal vocabulary at $\theta = 0$ and $\theta = 2\pi$, a
permutation null at $p < 0.001$, an in-plane causal-patch test
that flips logits in the predicted direction, and a blind causal
scan that rediscovers the same heads without consulting decode.
The same five tests pass on GPT-2 Small, Phi-3 Mini, Gemma-2-2B,
and Llama-3.2-3B --- two orders of magnitude in parameters and
three distinct training pipelines. We stop short of
``causal axis'' because the random-plane null returns
$p > 0.10$, with random-plane mean signals clustering at
$20$--$30$. We read this as gender being encoded over a
distributed 2D subspace of the late-layer residual; the OV-SVD
compass is a weight-readable coordinate system inside that
subspace rather than its unique axis. Amplitude linearity in
$\alpha$ ($R^2 \geq 0.99$) is consistent with the linear
injection itself; phase stability is the non-trivial test that
distinguishes a causal plane from a generic 2D direction.

\paragraph{Downstream behaviour.}
On held-out CrowS-Pairs (50/50 split, dev-selected multiplier
$M^*$ scored on test), Phi-3 gender at $M^* = 0.50$ lands at
test $|\mathrm{SS}-50| = 5.73$, against a dev-half baseline of
$|\mathrm{SS}-50| = 8.78$ at $M = 0$. The procedure also admits
$M^*=0$, dev-selected on $10$ of $16$ cells; on cells whose dev
baseline is genuinely near $50$ this generalises to small test
$|\mathrm{SS}-50|$ (GPT-2 race $0.00$, Gemma gender $0.38$, Llama
race $2.71$, Llama gender $3.44$). Fixed-projector debiasing
methods have no analogous null option.

\paragraph{Contributions.}
\begin{enumerate}
\itemsep0pt
\item \textbf{The compass primitive.} A probe-free 2D causal dial
read from $W_{OV}$ by SVD, with a three-test calibration recipe
(decode antipodes, phase-stable $\alpha$-sweep, $2\pi$-cyclic
decode) and a five-test pre-registered falsification battery
(\S\ref{sec:method}).
\item \textbf{Cross-architecture replication on four model
families.} Compasses with phase drift $\leq 3^\circ$ across
$\alpha \in \{1, 3, 10\}$, permutation null $p < 0.001$, and
$2\pi$-cyclic antipodal decode hold on GPT-2 Small (124M),
Phi-3 Mini (3.8B), Gemma-2-2B (2.6B), and Llama-3.2-3B (3.2B) ---
two orders of magnitude in parameters and three distinct training
recipes (\S\ref{sec:results:gender},
\cref{tab:gender,tab:battery}).
\item \textbf{Geometric structure beyond rank-1.} A four-way
temporal dial in Phi-3 L24H17 with second-harmonic
$A_2/A_1 = 0.32$ at $\alpha = 10$ vs.\ $\leq 0.06$ on the rank-2
entity dial (\S\ref{sec:results:harmonic}); a quantitative
storage-vs-steering dissociation across heads
(\S\ref{sec:results:storage}); and two distinct compasses on the
same model's gender axis at near-orthogonal principal angles
($86.4^\circ$, $89.0^\circ$ between Phi-3 L24H10 and L28H1,
\S\ref{sec:results:phi3two}).
\item \textbf{A held-out routed downstream procedure.}
Per-domain head routing, SNR-calibrated $\alpha$, and dev-selected
multiplier with $M = 0$ as a valid choice; the calibration recipe
makes per-cell $\alpha$ comparable across models that differ by
two orders of magnitude in singular-value scale
(\S\ref{sec:downstream:crows}, \cref{tab:crows_holdout}).
\end{enumerate}
