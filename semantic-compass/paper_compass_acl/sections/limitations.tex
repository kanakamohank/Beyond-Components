The OV-SVD compass passes the joint phase-stability and cyclicity
tests, but the random-plane null returns $p > 0.10$ on every primary
head (Holm-corrected; \cref{tab:holm}); we read the plane as a
weight-readable 2D slice of a distributed gender subspace rather
than a unique axis, and a direct test --- decoding the random
planes that exceed each head's observed statistic --- remains
future work. Our gender evaluation uses binary English pronoun
contrasts; non-binary expression and language-specific pronoun
systems are out of scope, and the cross-lingual decode tokens in
\cref{app:decode} are vocabulary overlap, not a within-language
causal evaluation.
