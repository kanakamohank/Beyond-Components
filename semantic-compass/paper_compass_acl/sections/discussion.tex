\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

\paragraph{What the rank-2 reading buys.}
Reading attention-head weights as 2D planes rather than as
collections of independent singular directions exposes structure
the single-direction view cannot. The Phi-3 temporal dial's
four-way harmonic ($A_2/A_1 = 0.32$ at $\alpha = 10$ vs.\
$\leq 0.06$ on the rank-2 two-way entity dial) is invisible to
single-axis decoding. The storage--steering dissociation
(\cref{tab:storage}) is recovered at the head level by reading
the pair $(A_{\mathrm{slope}}, |\Delta_{\mathrm{patch}}|)$ jointly.
The held-out routed CrowS-Pairs procedure with an $M = 0$
off-switch supports graded categorical steering at low
language-quality cost.

\paragraph{Adjacent comparisons.}
Three follow-ups would discipline the positive findings: DAS
\citep{geiger2024das} on the same head (does a learned 2D rotation
recover $(u_i, u_j)$?); an Engels-style helix probe
\citep{engels2024not} on Phi-3 L24H17 (does the temporal dial
occupy the same subspace as a probe-derived helix?); and a
sparse-autoencoder decomposition
\citep{cunningham2023sparse,templeton2024scaling} of the same head
(do two SAE features align with the compass plane?). All three are
natural extensions of the geometric reading we report.
