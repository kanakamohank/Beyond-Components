\section{Background and related work}
\label{sec:background}

\paragraph{OV circuit and singular-direction analysis.}
For a decoder head, $W_{OV} = W_V W_O \in \mathbb{R}^{d_{\text{model}}
\times d_{\text{model}}}$ \citep{elhage2021mathematical}. With
residuals as row vectors the head adds $r W_{OV}$ to the
destination token; taking $W_{OV} = U \Sigma V^\top$ gives
$r W_{OV} = r U \Sigma V^\top$, so the rows of $V^\top$ are the
write-out directions in residual coordinates. We let
$u_i := V^\top_{[i,:]}$. \citet{millidge2022svd} document that
these singular directions are often individually interpretable
through $W_U$. \citet{ahmad2025beyond} extract sparse rank-1
direction-level circuits via learnable masks on the singular
values for the GP, IOI, and Greater-Than tasks.

We extend the analysis from individual directions to the 2D plane
they span. The plane supports a continuous-angle injection
$\alpha\sigma(\cos\theta\, u_i + \sin\theta\, u_j)$, which we
characterise by phase stability across $\alpha$ together with a
second-harmonic test that separates two-way antipodal axes from
four-way categorical structure (\S\ref{sec:results:harmonic}).

\paragraph{Linear features in activation space.}
The linear representation hypothesis \citep{park2024linearrep}, DAS
\citep{geiger2024das}, geometry-of-truth probes
\citep{marks2024geometry}, sparse autoencoders
\citep{cunningham2023sparse,templeton2024scaling}, and function
vectors \citep{todd2024function} all search activation space for
low-dimensional causal features. We search the weight matrix
instead, which buys head-locality and removes probe training. The
trade is that we cannot find features the head does not natively
write through $W_{OV}$. DAS in particular is the closest neighbor:
both methods identify a 2D rotation underlying behavior; DAS
\emph{learns} the rotation under causal abstraction objectives, the
compass \emph{reads} it from the OV SVD. We do not run DAS as a
baseline; doing so would discipline our claim that the SVD-derived
plane is the right one.

\paragraph{Helices and circular features.}
\citet{engels2024not} show LMs encode certain numerical concepts as
approximate helices in the residual, recovered by trained probes.
Our temporal dial in Phi-3 L24H17 (\S\ref{sec:results:harmonic}) is
a categorical analog read from weights without probe training; an
explicit head-to-head---do Engels' months/days helix and our L24H17
plane occupy the same subspace?---would settle whether we are
finding a different object or a re-derivation of the same one.

\paragraph{Steering primitives.}
ROME \citep{meng2022rome}, ActAdd \citep{turner2023actadd}, and
Inference-Time Intervention \citep{li2023inference} are 1D additive
steering directions. The compass is a rank-2 generalization with a
continuous-$\theta$ family, but---per the random-plane null
result---we do not yet have evidence the rank-2 dial supports
qualitatively new affordances over scalar 1D steering. The
storage--steering dissociation (\S\ref{sec:results:storage})
suggests one place where it might.

\paragraph{Bias-evaluation context.}
We evaluate downstream behaviour on WinoGender
\citep{rudinger2018winogender,zhao2018winogender}, intra-sentence
StereoSet \citep{nadeem2021stereoset}, and CrowS-Pairs
\citep{nangia2020crowspairs}, with INLP \citep{ravfogel2020inlp}
and SentenceDebias \citep{liang2020sentdebias} as the natural
direction-based debiasing baselines. Two methodological caveats
shape our reporting: \citet{gonen2019lipstick} show linear-direction
debiasing can move surface metrics while leaving downstream signals
in nearby clusters, and \citet{blodgett2021stereotyping} document
annotation noise in StereoSet and CrowS-Pairs of order a few SS
points. We therefore present compass rotation as a graded
steering primitive with an off-switch (\S\ref{sec:downstream:crows})
rather than as a debiasing method, and read movement magnitudes
in light of the benchmark noise floor.
