\section{Prompt Lists}
\label{app:prompts}

All causal $\alpha$-sweeps average over three prompts per condition;
prompts are short and semantically ambiguous to keep baseline logit
separations small.

\paragraph{Gender (GPT-2, Phi-3, Gemma, Llama).}
Neutral: \emph{``The person said that''}, \emph{``Then they said that''},
\emph{``Afterwards, the speaker said that''}.
$+$-context (masculine): \emph{``The man laced up his boots because''},
\emph{``The father waved to the crowd and''}, \emph{``The king announced that''}.
$-$-context (feminine): \emph{``The woman laced up her boots because''},
\emph{``The mother waved to the crowd and''}, \emph{``The queen announced that''}.

\paragraph{Temporal (Phi-3 L24H17, $t_+$ = \emph{month}, $t_-$ = \emph{hour}).}
Neutral: \emph{``The event took about one''}, \emph{``They waited
nearly one''}, \emph{``The project finished within one''}.
$+$ (month-biasing): \emph{``The rent is due every''}, \emph{``Pregnancy
lasts about nine''}, \emph{``The magazine arrives once a''}.
$-$ (hour-biasing): \emph{``The meeting lasts exactly one''}, \emph{``The
drive takes roughly one''}, \emph{``She slept for a single''}.

\paragraph{Entity (Phi-3 L24H28, $t_+$ = \emph{item}, $t_-$ = \emph{operation}).}
Neutral: \emph{``The list contained a single''}, \emph{``He removed
exactly one''}, \emph{``The inventory showed one''}.
$+$ (item-biasing): \emph{``On the shelf sat a small''}, \emph{``The
box held only one''}, \emph{``She picked up a single''}.
$-$ (operation-biasing): \emph{``The surgeon completed a complex''},
\emph{``The factory ran one more''}, \emph{``The team executed a risky''}.

\section{Battery Implementation}
\label{app:battery_details}

\paragraph{Random-plane null.} For each primary head, we draw $1000$
random orthonormal pairs $(u_i', u_j')$ from
$\mathbb{R}^{d_{\text{model}}}$ via QR decomposition of Gaussian
matrices, recompute the discriminative statistic
$D = \overline{\mathrm{LD}_{+}} - \overline{\mathrm{LD}_{-}}$ at
$\alpha = 1$, and report the empirical $p = \Pr(D_{\text{rand}} \ge
D_{\text{obs}})$.

\paragraph{Permutation null.} For each head we permute the
$\{+,-\}$ context label across prompts $2000$ times and compute the
same $D$. The permutation null tests whether the signal could come
from random label assignment; it returns $p < 0.001$ for every primary
head we test.

\paragraph{Cyclicity.} We project $u_i + i u_j$ onto the unit circle
in $\mathbb{C}$ and read off the top-$10$ tokens at $\theta =
0, \pi/2, \pi, 3\pi/2, 2\pi$ via $W_U^\top$. The verdict
\emph{CIRCLE: closed and with distinct antipodes} requires Jaccard
identity at $\theta = 0$ and $\theta = 2\pi$, and Jaccard zero
between $\theta = 0$ and $\theta = \pi$. All five primary heads
satisfy this.

\paragraph{Causal-patch.} For a probe prompt, we cache the residual
at the head's layer, project onto the compass plane, rotate the
in-plane projection by $\Delta \in \{\pm 90^\circ, \pm 180^\circ\}$
preserving the magnitude, and replace the residual with the rotated
version on the last token only. We report the maximum absolute
logit shift across rotations.

\section{Two-pillar thresholds}
\label{app:thresholds}

The blind scan applies three thresholds: $R^2 \ge 0.95$ on the
linearity fit, $\Delta\phi \le 10^\circ$ on the phase across
$\alpha \in \{3, 10\}$, and $A_{\text{slope}} = A(10)/10 \ge 0.20$.
For Gemma-2-2B, we lower $A_{\text{slope}}$ to $0.08$ because the
post-pre-norm residual at the layer of interest is normalized to a
larger magnitude than in the other three models, so the per-unit
$\alpha$ writes a smaller fraction of the residual; the natural
$\alpha\sigma$ injection (rather than unit-norm injection) recovers
the same compass at the higher slope. The threshold change does not
add or remove heads from the four-model story.

(month / upon / hour / year); Phi-3 L24H28 (entity) scores
$\le 0.06$, consistent with two superposed antipodal axes.

\section{Per-domain $\alpha$ calibration}
\label{app:alpha_calib}

We calibrate $\alpha_d = \tau / \overline{\mathrm{snr}}_h$ where
$\mathrm{snr}_h = \|v_h\| / \|r_{\text{pre}}\|$ at the head's layer
on a neutral prompt, and $\tau$ is a model-specific target SNR. The
calibrated $\alpha_d$ values used for $M = 1$ in \cref{tab:crows_holdout}
are: GPT-2 ($\tau = 0.20$): $1.89/1.80/1.37/1.87$ for
gender/race/profession/religion. Phi-3 ($\tau = 0.08$):
$5.10/5.15/3.80/5.34$. Gemma ($\tau = 0.08$):
$55.26/81.36/29.73/31.19$ (Gemma's smaller singular values require
larger $\alpha$ to match SNR). Llama ($\tau = 0.10$):
$4.06/3.56/3.45/4.66$.
